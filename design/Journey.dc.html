VISITOR JOURNEY · THE WHOLE SITE AS ONE CALL

Roots and vines are the patch cables, the data river carries packets, tree rings count the years. Each chapter has one thing to do, and doing it lights up the next line.

NEVER TRAPPED

Every gate is a soft gate: scrolling always continues, and the switchboard map jumps to any chapter.

ONE SCENE PER SCREEN ON PHONES

Chapters snap to the viewport; swipe up continues, toys use big tap targets.

THE CABLE IS THE THREAD

One live cable carries the visitor from chapter to chapter and doubles as the progress bar.

REMEMBER THE CALL

Visited chapters stay lit on the map; returning visitors resume where they hung up.

RECRUITER FAST LANE

A ‘Skip the call — show me the CV’ link is always one tap away.

THE WORLD IS THE BACKGROUND

Three parallax layers per world (sky, far, near) that cross-fade into the next chapter as the visitor walks; phones get two layers and no parallax on low-power.

CALM MODE

With reduced motion on, each chapter shows its world as a still backdrop — same content, no movement.
